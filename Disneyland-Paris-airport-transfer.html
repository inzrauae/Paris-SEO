Before You Fly

Tips for Your Shuttle to Disneyland Paris

A little preparation makes the start of your Disney holiday smooth and stress-free.

  • Share your flight number. We track your flight, so your driver is there when you land, even if you are delayed.
  • Give your hotel name. Tell us which Disneyland Paris hotel you are staying at, or that you are going to the park entrance.
  • Request child seats. Child seats are available upon request, so tell us the ages of your children when you book.
  • Tell us your group size. A private shuttle suits families and groups travelling with luggage.

FAQ

Shuttle Bus Disneyland Paris: Common Questions

Is there a shuttle bus to Disneyland Paris from the airport?

Shared shuttle buses to Disneyland Paris exist, but PFT Cabs offers a private shuttle instead: your own cab or van picks you up at CDG, Orly or Beauvais and takes you directly to your Disneyland Paris hotel or the park entrance, with no shared stops.

What is the difference between a shuttle bus and a private shuttle to Disneyland Paris?

A shared shuttle bus runs on a schedule and may stop at several hotels. A private shuttle is pre-booked for your group only: the driver meets you at arrivals, tracks your flight and takes you straight to your destination.

Which airports do you serve for Disneyland Paris transfers?

We provide private transfers from Charles de Gaulle (CDG), Orly (ORY) and Beauvais (BVA) airports directly to Disneyland Paris hotels or parks.

How do I book a private shuttle to Disneyland Paris?

Book online on this website, call +33 752 24 44 44 or message PFT Cabs on WhatsApp. You can reserve in advance for any date and time.

What if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight and wait for you even if it is delayed. Please share your flight number when you book.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes. Spacious, air-conditioned cars are available with child seats upon request. Please tell us when you book.

Can you take me to my Disneyland Paris hotel or the park entrance?

Yes. We take you directly to your Disneyland Paris hotel or the park entrance. Share your hotel name when you book.

How long does the journey to Disneyland Paris take?

From Charles de Gaulle Airport it takes approximately 40–50 minutes and from Orly Airport approximately 45–55 minutes, depending on traffic.

Do you offer transfers between Paris and Disneyland Paris?

Yes. We also run private transfers between Paris and Disneyland Paris. See our Paris to Disneyland transfers page for details.
