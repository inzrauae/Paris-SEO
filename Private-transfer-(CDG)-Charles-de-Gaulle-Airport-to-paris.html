CDG to Paris Transfer at a Glance

PFT Cabs (Paris Fast Transfer) offers a private CDG to Paris transfer: a pre-booked taxi or chauffeur car from Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG) to any hotel, apartment or address in Paris. Your driver meets you at arrivals and takes you directly to your destination, with no queue for a taxi and no train change.

Arriving in Paris? Avoid long taxi queues and unreliable rides. Our CDG to Paris transfer from Charles de Gaulle Airport ensures a smooth, fixed-rate journey directly to your hotel or apartment. Meet your driver at the arrivals area and travel in comfort.

Before You Fly

Tips for Your CDG to Paris Transfer

A little preparation makes your arrival at Charles de Gaulle Airport smooth and stress-free.

  • Share your flight number. Your driver tracks your flight, so the pickup stays on time even if you land early or late.
  • Give the full Paris address. Include the hotel name or street and number so the driver can take you straight to the door.
  • Tell us your group size and bags. We recommend a business sedan, premium van or family minibus to match.
  • Look for your name board. After you collect your luggage, your driver meets you at the arrivals area.

About Charles de Gaulle Airport

  • Code: CDG, the main international airport serving Paris
  • Location: north-east of Paris, roughly 25 km from the city centre
  • Transfer time: approximately 35–50 minutes by road, depending on traffic
  • Terminals: Terminal 1, Terminal 2 and Terminal 3. Add yours to your booking.

FAQ

CDG to Paris Transfer: Common Questions

How long does a CDG to Paris transfer take?

A CDG to Paris transfer takes approximately 35–50 minutes, depending on traffic and your destination in the city.

Is the CDG to Paris transfer a fixed rate?

Yes. Our private transfer from Charles de Gaulle Airport to Paris is a fixed-rate journey directly to your hotel or apartment. See the taxi rates page for current prices.

Where will my driver meet me at CDG Airport?

Your driver meets you at the arrivals area with a name board and helps with your luggage.

How do I book a private transfer from CDG to Paris?

Book online on this website, call +33 752 24 44 44 or message PFT Cabs on WhatsApp. You can reserve in advance for any date and time.

How far is CDG Airport from central Paris?

Charles de Gaulle Airport is located north-east of Paris, roughly 25 km from the city centre. A private transfer takes approximately 35–50 minutes depending on traffic.

What is the difference between a private CDG to Paris transfer and public transport?

A private transfer is door-to-door: your driver meets you at arrivals and takes you directly to your Paris hotel or address with your luggage, without changing trains or waiting in a taxi queue.

What happens if my flight to CDG is delayed?

Your driver tracks your flight, so the pickup is adjusted to your actual arrival time. Please share your flight number when you book your CDG to Paris transfer.

Can I book a private transfer from Paris to CDG Airport?

Yes. We also run private Paris to CDG transfers. Book your pickup time and address in advance and your driver collects you in time for your flight.

Which vehicle should I choose for my CDG to Paris transfer?

Choose a business sedan for couples or small groups, a premium van for more passengers or luggage, or a family minibus for larger groups. All vehicles are air-conditioned.

Can I get a private taxi from CDG to a Paris train station?

Yes. We take passengers from Charles de Gaulle Airport to hotels, apartments and train stations in Paris. Tell us the station name when you book.
