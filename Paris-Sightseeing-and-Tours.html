Must-See Paris

The Most Popular Landmarks to Visit in Paris

Combine your favourite sights in one comfortable day with a private driver.

Eiffel Tower

No Paris visit is complete without the iconic Eiffel Tower. Enjoy stunning views from the Champ de Mars or head to the top for breathtaking panoramic sights.

Louvre Museum

Home to world-famous artworks like the Mona Lisa and Venus de Milo, a must-visit for art and history lovers. A private cab ensures early and comfortable access.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

Admire the beautiful Gothic architecture and intricate details of this historic Parisian landmark.

Montmartre & Sacré-Cœur

Explore the artistic streets of Montmartre and enjoy stunning city views from the Sacré-Cœur Basilica.

Champs-Élysées & Arc de Triomphe

Walk along the elegant Champs-Élysées, lined with shops and cafés, and visit the Arc de Triomphe for a spectacular city view.

Palace of Versailles

Located near Paris, Versailles showcases royal luxury with its magnificent gardens and the famous Hall of Mirrors. A private cab makes this trip smooth and relaxing.

Off the Beaten Path

Hidden Gems to Explore in Paris

Charming corners away from the busiest tourist areas.

Le Marais District

Known for historic streets, trendy boutiques and lively cafés, Le Marais is perfect for a relaxed afternoon.

Le Bon Marché

A premium shopping destination offering authentic French fashion, gourmet food and luxury products.

Canal Saint-Martin

Enjoy a peaceful walk along the canal, surrounded by local shops and charming restaurants.

FAQ

Paris Sightseeing Tours: Common Questions

What are private Paris sightseeing tours?

Private Paris sightseeing tours with PFT Cabs are chauffeur-driven trips around the city in a comfortable car, on a custom itinerary built around the attractions you want to see, with door-to-door pickup and drop-off.

How do I book a Paris sightseeing tour?

Book online on this website, call +33 752 24 44 44 or message PFT Cabs on WhatsApp. Tell us your favourite attractions so we can plan your tour.

Can I choose my own itinerary?

Yes. We offer custom itineraries, so you can plan your tour based on your favourite attractions, such as the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre, Montmartre or Versailles.

Do you go to the Palace of Versailles?

Yes. The Palace of Versailles is located near Paris, and a private cab makes the trip smooth and relaxing.

Which Paris landmarks can I visit?

Popular stops include the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral, Montmartre and Sacré-Cœur Basilica, the Champs-Élysées and the Arc de Triomphe, plus hidden gems like Le Marais, Le Bon Marché and Canal Saint-Martin.

Why take a private car instead of public transport?

A private car offers door-to-door pickup and drop-off, a flexible itinerary, clean and spacious comfort, and no crowded public transport or long waiting times.

Is the tour suitable for families and solo travellers?

Yes. Our comfortable rides are ideal for families, couples and solo travellers.

Are the drivers experienced?

Yes. Our drivers are experienced and polite and know the best routes around Paris.

Can you also take me to or from the airport?

Yes. We run private airport transfers from CDG, Orly and Beauvais, so you can combine your arrival transfer with your sightseeing plans.
