Nearby Hotels Around Disneyland Paris

Planning a magical trip to Disneyland Paris? Choosing the right hotel near the park can greatly enhance your overall experience. From Disney-themed resorts to family-friendly hotels, there are plenty of excellent accommodation options located just minutes away from the park.

Popular Hotels Near Disneyland Paris

Disney’s Hotel Santa Fe: A budget-friendly option featuring a fun Cars-themed atmosphere inspired by Route 66. Perfect for families with kids who love adventure and color.

Disney’s Hotel New York – The Art of Marvel: A dream stay for Marvel fans, offering modern rooms and premium suites. The hotel features Marvel-inspired décor, exclusive artwork, and plenty of kid-friendly amenities.

Disney’s Hotel Cheyenne: Step into the Wild West at this Toy Story-themed hotel. Enjoy a cowboy-style setting where beloved characters add extra magic to your stay.

Disney’s Sequoia Lodge: Nestled in a forest-inspired environment, this cozy 3-star lodge is ideal for travelers seeking relaxation, warmth, and a nature-themed retreat.

Disney’s Newport Bay Club: A grand lakeside hotel with elegant nautical décor, located just minutes from the park. Guests can enjoy refined dining, including the popular yacht-style restaurant.

Vienna House Magic Circus: A charming circus-themed hotel offering spacious family rooms and a fairytale atmosphere, perfect for a magical family getaway.

B&B Hotel Disneyland Paris: A comfortable and modern hotel from a trusted international chain. It offers free shuttle services to Disneyland Paris and is ideal for travelers seeking value and convenience.

Hotel Facilities & Entertainment

These hotels provide a wide range of room types, from standard rooms to luxury suites, along with essential facilities such as free Wi-Fi, private bathrooms, and daily housekeeping. Guests can also enjoy entertainment services including kids’ play areas, meet-and-greet sessions with Disney characters, and restaurants serving international cuisine.
