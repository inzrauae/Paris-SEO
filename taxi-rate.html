Paris Airport Taxi Prices at a Glance

Paris Fast Transfer (PFT Cabs) offers private airport taxis with fixed prices between Charles de Gaulle (CDG), Orly (ORY), Beauvais (BVA), Paris and Disneyland Paris. Taxi Price FAQ

How much is a taxi from CDG airport to Paris?

A private taxi from Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG) to Paris starts from €80 for 3 passengers, €100 for 7 passengers and €185 for 9 passengers. Prices are fixed for your route and passenger count.

How much is a taxi from Orly airport to Paris?

A private taxi from Paris Orly Airport (ORY) to Paris starts from €75 for 3 passengers, €95 for 7 passengers and €175 for 9 passengers.

How much is a taxi from Beauvais airport to Paris?

A private taxi from Beauvais-Tillé Airport (BVA) to Paris starts from €160 for 3 passengers, €175 for 7 passengers and €330 for 9 passengers. Beauvais is farther from Paris than CDG and Orly, so the fare is higher.

How much is a taxi from the airport to Disneyland Paris?

Private transfers to Disneyland Paris start from €75 from CDG, €85 from Orly and €160 from Beauvais for 3 passengers. The price rises with the number of passengers; see the table above for 3 to 9 passengers.

How much is a taxi from Disneyland Paris to Paris?

A private taxi from Disneyland Paris to Paris starts from €90 for 3 passengers, €115 for 7 passengers and €180 for 9 passengers.

Are the taxi prices fixed?

Yes. Paris Fast Transfer works with fixed prices for each route and passenger count, so you know the fare before you travel.

What if my route or group size is not in the table?

Contact Paris Fast Transfer on +33 752 24 44 44, WhatsApp or the contact page for a quote on other routes or larger groups. The live booking form also shows the price for your route before you confirm.
