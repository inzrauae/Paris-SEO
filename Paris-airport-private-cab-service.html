Airport Pickups

Private Car From Each Paris Airport

All three Paris airports are covered. Journey times depend on traffic, so we recommend allowing extra time.

Charles de Gaulle (CDG)

The main international airport, roughly 25 km north-east of central Paris. A private car to Paris typically takes about 35 to 50 minutes. See CDG airport private taxi and CDG to Disneyland Paris.

Orly (ORY)

About 13 km south of the city and the closest to central Paris. See Orly to Paris and Orly to Disneyland Paris.

Beauvais (BVA)

About 85 km from Paris and used mainly by low-cost airlines, so a pre-booked car is especially useful. See our Beauvais taxi page.

Private Car Service Paris, France: Common Questions

What is a private car service in Paris, France?

A private car service in Paris is a pre-booked car with a professional chauffeur for airport pickups, drop-offs and intercity rides. With PFT Cabs you get fixed prices, clean luxury vehicles, free Wi-Fi and flight tracking, available 24/7.

How do I book a private car in Paris?

Book online on this website, call +33 752 24 44 44 or message PFT Cabs on WhatsApp. You can reserve in advance for any date and time.

Which Paris airports does the private car service cover?

We provide private car transfers to and from all Paris airports, including Charles de Gaulle (CDG), Orly (ORY) and Beauvais (BVA).

Are the prices fixed?

Yes. We offer fixed prices, so you know the cost of your private car before you travel. See the taxi rates page for current prices.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight and wait for you with no extra cost for delays. Please share your flight number when you book.

Is the private car service available 24/7?

Yes. We are available 24/7 for airport pickups, drop-offs and intercity rides.

What comes with the private car?

All our vehicles are clean, air-conditioned and equipped with free Wi-Fi, driven by friendly and experienced chauffeurs.

Can I book a private car for an intercity trip from Paris?

Yes. Besides airport transfers we offer intercity rides. See our private transfers across France page for longer journeys from Paris.

What is the difference between a private car and a regular taxi?

A private car is pre-booked with a fixed price and a chauffeur who tracks your flight and meets you on arrival. A regular taxi is usually taken from the queue on arrival, with the fare metered or set by the operator.

Is there a private car service in Paris, France for airport transfers?

Yes. PFT Cabs (Paris Fast Transfer) runs a private car service in Paris, France, with pre-booked chauffeur-driven cars between Charles de Gaulle (CDG), Orly (ORY) and Beauvais (BVA) airports, central Paris, hotels and Disneyland Paris, available 24/7.

How much does a private car service in Paris, France cost?

Prices are fixed for each route and passenger count. For example, a private car from CDG to Paris starts from €80 for 3 passengers and from Orly to Paris from €75 for 3 passengers. See the taxi rates page for the full price table and current prices.

Can I book a private car service from Paris to other cities in France?

Yes. Besides airport and city transfers, PFT Cabs offers longer journeys from Paris across France, such as Normandy, the Loire Valley, Champagne and Mont-Saint-Michel. See the France transfer page, or contact us for a quote on your route.

What vehicles can I book for a private car in Paris?

You can request premium cars such as Mercedes-Benz and BMW, or a minivan for families and larger groups, depending on availability. Tell PFT Cabs your passenger numbers and luggage when you book so we can recommend the right vehicle.

Can I book a child seat with my private car?

Child seats are available on request. Give the age of each child when you book so a suitable seat can be arranged, subject to confirmation.

Will my driver meet me at the airport?

Yes. Your driver meets you on arrival and helps with your luggage. Share your flight number when you book so we can track your flight and keep your pickup on time.

How many passengers can travel in one private car?

Our published prices cover groups of 3 to 9 passengers, with a larger vehicle for bigger groups. For larger groups or special luggage, contact PFT Cabs for a quote.
