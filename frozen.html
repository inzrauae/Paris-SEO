Discover the Magical World of Frozen at Disneyland Paris

By Paris Fast Transfer · Updated 19 October 2026

Step into the enchanting Kingdom of Arendelle at World of Frozen at Disneyland Paris, where the beloved Disney films come to life through immersive scenery, magical attractions, and unforgettable character experiences. Inspired by Disney's Frozen and Frozen 2, this themed land invites visitors to explore the world of Elsa, Anna, Olaf, and their friends in a spectacular Nordic-inspired setting.

Officially opened on 29 March 2026, World of Frozen is located inside Disney Adventure World, the second park at Disneyland Paris. It brings the landscapes, architecture, and atmosphere of Arendelle to life, offering families and Disney fans an opportunity to experience the Frozen story in a new way.

Whether you are planning a family holiday, visiting Disneyland Paris for the first time, or travelling with fellow Frozen fans, this magical destination deserves a place on your itinerary.

What to Expect at World of Frozen

World of Frozen recreates the fictional Kingdom of Arendelle with detailed architecture, themed surroundings, character experiences, and attractions inspired by the films. From the village streets to Elsa's spectacular Ice Palace, the environment is designed to immerse visitors in the Frozen universe.

Frozen Ever After: A Magical Boat Ride

The main attraction is Frozen Ever After, a family-friendly musical boat ride that takes visitors on a journey through Arendelle.

During the experience, you travel through themed landscapes, encounter familiar characters, and journey towards Elsa's Ice Palace on the North Mountain. Music, visual effects, and carefully designed scenery bring memorable moments from the Frozen films to life.

The attraction has no minimum height requirement, making it an option for families with younger children. However, visitors should check the official accessibility guidance and current operating schedule before their visit.

Meet Elsa and Anna

Meeting Elsa and Anna can be a highlight of a Disneyland Paris holiday. World of Frozen offers a Royal Encounter experience inside Arendelle Castle, providing visitors with an opportunity to meet the sisters and capture special photographs.

Reservations are required for this particular character encounter, and availability is limited. Check the official Disneyland Paris mobile app for booking instructions and the latest character appearance information.

Enjoy A Celebration in Arendelle

Visitors can also experience A Celebration in Arendelle, a themed outdoor show featuring beloved Frozen characters and festive scenes inspired by the kingdom's traditions.

Show schedules and character appearances can vary, so consult the official park app on the day of your visit to plan your time effectively.

Discover the Village and Themed Shops

Beyond the main attraction, World of Frozen features carefully designed buildings, picturesque village streets, and themed shopping experiences.

Visitors can browse Frozen-inspired toys, collectibles, and souvenirs at the area's themed boutiques. These shops offer an opportunity to find keepsakes that celebrate the characters and stories of Arendelle.

Themed dining options also provide opportunities to take a break during your visit.

Plan Your Visit to World of Frozen

World of Frozen is part of Disney Adventure World, so you need valid admission to that park to experience the themed land. Access to World of Frozen is included with admission to Disney Adventure World; a separate World of Frozen ticket is not required. However, certain character encounters may require reservations.

For a smoother visit, consider the following tips:

  • Check park opening hours before travelling.
  • Review attraction wait times through the official Disneyland Paris app.
  • Check the availability and reservation requirements for character encounters.
  • Allow enough time for Frozen Ever After, shopping, dining, and photographs.
  • Book popular experiences in advance where possible.
  • Plan your transportation before your visit, particularly if you are travelling with children or arriving directly from an airport.
