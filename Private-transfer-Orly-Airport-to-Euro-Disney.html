Before You Fly

Tips for Your Orly to Disneyland Transfer

A little preparation makes the start of your Disney holiday smooth and stress-free.

  • Share your flight number. Your driver tracks your flight, so the pickup stays on time if you land early or late.
  • Give your hotel name. Tell us which Disneyland Paris hotel you are staying at, or that you are going to the park entrance.
  • Request child seats. Child seats are available upon request, so let us know the ages of your children when you book.
  • Allow for traffic. The 45–55 minute journey depends on traffic, so leave extra time when travelling back to Orly for your flight.

FAQ

Orly to Disneyland Transfer: Common Questions

What is an Orly to Disneyland transfer?

An Orly to Disneyland transfer is a pre-booked private ride from Orly Airport directly to your Disneyland Paris hotel or the park entrance. With PFT Cabs your driver tracks your flight, meets you at the arrivals hall and drives you straight there in a clean, air-conditioned cab.

How long does the transfer from Orly to Disneyland Paris take?

The transfer from Orly Airport to Disneyland Paris takes approximately 45–55 minutes, depending on traffic conditions.

How do I book an Orly to Disneyland transfer?

Book online on this website, call +33 752 24 44 44 or message PFT Cabs on WhatsApp. You can reserve in advance for any date and time.

Will the driver meet me at Orly Airport?

Yes. Your polite, English-speaking chauffeur tracks your flight and meets you at the arrivals hall.

Do you provide child seats for the Orly to Disneyland transfer?

Yes. Child seats are available upon request. Please tell us when you book.

What vehicles can I choose for the transfer?

You can choose a sedan, a luxury van or a family vehicle, depending on your group size and luggage.

Is the Orly to Disneyland Paris transfer price fixed?

Yes. We offer fixed pricing, so you know the cost before you travel, with no waiting. See the taxi rates page for current prices.

Can I book a return transfer from Disneyland Paris to Orly?

Yes. You can pre-book a private transfer from Disneyland Paris back to Orly Airport so you arrive in time for your flight.

Do you also offer transfers from CDG or Paris to Disneyland Paris?

Yes. We run private transfers from Charles de Gaulle Airport to Disneyland Paris and between Paris and the park. See our CDG to Disneyland Paris and Paris to Disneyland pages for details.
