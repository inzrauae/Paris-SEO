FAQ

France Transfer FAQ

Do you offer private transfers outside Paris and across France?

Yes. PFT Cabs arranges private transfers from Paris and its airports to destinations across France, such as Normandy, the Loire Valley, Champagne and Mont-Saint-Michel. Contact us with your route and date and we will confirm your trip.

How do I book a France transfer with PFT Cabs?

Send us your pickup point, destination, date and number of passengers using the booking form, by phone on +33 752 24 44 44 or on WhatsApp. We confirm the details of your transfer before the trip.

Can I be picked up at CDG, Orly or Beauvais airport for a long-distance trip?

Yes. Long-distance transfers can start at Charles de Gaulle, Orly or Beauvais airport, or at a hotel or address in Paris.

Is customer support available 24/7?

Yes, our customer support is available 24/7 by phone, email and WhatsApp.

Which destinations can I reach from Paris?

Popular destinations include Normandy (Rouen, Giverny, Honfleur, Deauville and the D-Day beaches), the Loire Valley (Chambord, Chenonceau, Amboise), Champagne (Reims and Épernay), Mont-Saint-Michel, Versailles and Fontainebleau, and Northern France (Lille, Amiens and the Opal Coast).

My destination is not listed. Can you still help?

Possibly. Contact us with your route and date and we will confirm whether we can arrange your transfer.

Do you offer day trips as well as one-way transfers?

Yes. For example, Mont-Saint-Michel is available as day trips and one-way transfers from Paris.

What is the difference between a private transfer and taking the train?

A private transfer is door to door: you are picked up at your airport, Paris hotel or address and driven directly to your destination by a professional driver, without arranging onward travel from a station.

Do you also offer transfers within Paris?

Yes. See our private car service in Paris, CDG airport private taxi, taxi Paris Orly and Disneyland airport cab service pages.

How long does it take to drive from Paris to Normandy, the Loire Valley, Champagne or Mont-Saint-Michel?

As a rough guide, Giverny takes about 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes, Rouen and Reims about 1.5 to 2 hours, Honfleur, Deauville and the Loire Valley about 2.5 to 3 hours, and Mont-Saint-Michel about 4 to 4.5 hours each way. Actual times depend on traffic and your exact route.

Can I travel from the airport directly to my destination in France?

Yes. Your transfer can start at Charles de Gaulle, Orly or Beauvais airport and go directly to your destination. Give us your flight details and destination so we can confirm your trip.

Can we stop along the way?

Plan your route with your driver in advance. Confirm any extra stops, waiting time and additional charges when you book so that everything is agreed before the trip.

How far in advance should I book a France transfer?

Long-distance transfers are arranged in advance, so contact us as early as you can with your route, date and number of passengers. We confirm your transfer details before the trip.
