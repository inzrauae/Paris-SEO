PFT Cabs (Paris Fast Transfer) is a private Paris cab service, with a car with chauffeur on demand. We offer private transfers from Paris airports including Charles de Gaulle, Orly and Beauvais to Disneyland Paris, city hotels and other destinations. Our Paris cab service covers airports and many other locations in the French capital.

For business or leisure travel, our Paris cab service comes with on-demand drivers. Our services include online booking, fixed rates and punctual pick-ups, whether you travel solo, with family or in a group.

FAQ

Our Services: Common Questions

What services does PFT Cabs offer?

PFT Cabs is a Paris cab service offering a car with chauffeur for airport transfers, Disneyland Paris transfers, city transport, sightseeing tours, business transport, transfers across France and online taxi booking.

Which airports do you serve?

We serve Charles de Gaulle (CDG), Orly (ORY) and Beauvais (BVA) airports, with transfers to Paris city, hotels and Disneyland Paris.

How do I book a service?

Book online using the booking form on our home page, call +33 752 24 44 44 or message PFT Cabs on WhatsApp. You can schedule in advance, choose your vehicle type and receive instant confirmation.

Do you offer fixed rates?

Yes. Our services include fixed rates. See the taxi rates page for current prices.

Are your drivers professional and English-speaking?

Yes. Our professional drivers are English-speaking and drive comfortable, well-maintained vehicles for individuals, families and groups.

Can I book a sightseeing tour or a transfer outside Paris?

Yes. We offer sightseeing tours with a private driver and private transfers from Paris across France. See our Paris sightseeing tours and France transfer pages.

Is customer support available 24/7?

Yes. Our customer support is available 24/7 by phone, email and WhatsApp.
