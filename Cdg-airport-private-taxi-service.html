Before You Fly

Tips for Your CDG Airport Private Taxi

A little preparation makes your arrival at Charles de Gaulle Airport smooth and stress-free.

  • Share your flight number. Your driver monitors your flight, so the pickup stays on time if you land early or late.
  • Give the full destination address. Include the hotel name or street and number so the driver can take you straight to the door.
  • Tell us your group size and bags. We will make sure you have the right car for your journey.
  • Add your terminal. Charles de Gaulle has Terminals 1, 2 and 3, so let us know where you arrive.

About Charles de Gaulle Airport

  • Code: CDG, the main international airport serving Paris
  • Location: north-east of Paris, roughly 25 km from the city centre
  • Terminals: Terminal 1, Terminal 2 and Terminal 3

FAQ

CDG Airport Private Taxi: Common Questions

What is a CDG airport private taxi?

A CDG airport private taxi is a pre-booked taxi with a professional driver who meets you at Charles de Gaulle Airport and takes you directly to your destination. With PFT Cabs you get flight tracking, fixed pricing and 24/7 availability, with no waiting in the taxi queue.

How do I book a private taxi at CDG Airport?

Book online on this website by entering your pickup and drop-off locations, call +33 752 24 44 44, or message PFT Cabs on WhatsApp. You can reserve in advance for any date and time.

Is a CDG airport private taxi available 24/7?

Yes. Our CDG private taxi service is available 24/7, so you can book a pickup for early-morning and late-night arrivals as well as daytime flights.

What happens if my flight to CDG is delayed?

Your driver monitors your flight and waits if it is delayed, at no extra charge. Please share your flight number when you book.

Is the CDG private taxi price fixed?

Yes. We offer fixed pricing, so you know the cost of your CDG private taxi before you travel. See the taxi rates page for current prices.

Where does the CDG private taxi take me?

We take you from Charles de Gaulle Airport to central Paris, hotels, apartments, train stations and Disneyland Paris. See our CDG to Paris transfer and CDG to Disneyland Paris pages for route details.

Can I book a private taxi from Paris to CDG Airport?

Yes. You can also pre-book a private taxi from your Paris address to Charles de Gaulle Airport and your driver collects you in time for your flight.

Do your CDG taxi drivers speak English?

Yes. Our drivers are friendly, English-speaking and know Paris well, so your journey from the airport is stress-free.

What comes with the ride?

Our cars are modern and air-conditioned, with free Wi-Fi and bottled water on board.
