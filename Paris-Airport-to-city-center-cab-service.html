Before You Fly

Tips for Your Airport to Paris City Center Transfer

A little preparation makes your arrival in Paris smooth and stress-free.

  • Share your flight details. Include your airport, flight number and terminal so your driver is ready when you land.
  • Give the full city address. Include the hotel name or street and number so the driver takes you straight to the door.
  • Tell us your group size and bags. We will make sure you have the right vehicle.
  • Allow for traffic. Journey time from the airport to the city center depends on the airport and traffic, so book your return trip with extra time.

FAQ

Paris Airport to City Center: Common Questions

How do I get from Paris airport to the city center?

You can pre-book a private cab from Paris airport to city center with PFT Cabs. Your driver picks you up at CDG, Orly or Beauvais and takes you directly to your hotel, apartment or address in Paris, with no waiting in the taxi queue.

Which Paris airports do you serve?

We provide cab services from Charles de Gaulle (CDG), Orly (ORY) and Beauvais (BVA) airports to Paris city center and other destinations.

How do I book a cab from the airport to Paris city center?

Book online on this website by entering your pickup and drop-off locations, call +33 752 24 44 44, or message PFT Cabs on WhatsApp. You can reserve in advance for any date and time.

Are the fares from the airport to the city center fixed?

Yes. We offer fixed fares, so you know the price of your airport to city center cab before you travel. See the taxi rates page for current prices.

Is the cab service available 24/7?

Yes. We provide 24/7 private cab services from Paris airports to the city center, including early-morning and late-night arrivals.

Which Paris airport is closest to the city center?

Orly is located south of Paris and is closer to the city center than Charles de Gaulle, which is north-east of the city. Beauvais is further away, so allow extra travel time. See our Compare Paris Airports page for details.

Can I book a cab from Paris city center to the airport?

Yes. You can also pre-book a private cab from your Paris address to CDG, Orly or Beauvais and your driver collects you in time for your flight.

Do your drivers speak English?

Yes. Our professional drivers are English-speaking and drive modern vehicles for a comfortable, relaxing ride.

Can you take me from the airport to a Paris train station or hotel?

Yes. We take passengers from Paris airports directly to hotels, apartments and train stations in the city center. Share the exact address or station name when you book.
