Which Paris Airport Is Best for You? CDG vs Orly vs Beauvais

By Paris Fast Transfer · Updated 19 October 2026

Paris is served by three major airports: Charles de Gaulle (CDG), Paris Orly (ORY), and Beauvais–Tillé (BVA). Each airport offers different advantages depending on your airline, flight schedule, budget, and final destination. Choosing the right airport can make your trip to Paris more convenient and help you plan your onward transportation efficiently.

Whether you are visiting the French capital, travelling to Disneyland Paris, or connecting between flights, understanding the differences between these airports can help you make the right decision. With Paris Fast Transfer, you can arrange private airport transportation and compare suitable transfer options for your journey.

Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG)

Charles de Gaulle is Paris's principal international airport and a popular arrival point for long-haul travellers. Located northeast of central Paris, it offers connections to numerous international destinations.

CDG can be a convenient choice for travellers heading to central Paris, Disneyland Paris, or other destinations in the surrounding region. Private transfers provide a direct alternative to public transport, particularly for families and passengers travelling with luggage.

Paris Orly Airport (ORY)

Orly Airport is located south of Paris and serves domestic, European, and selected international routes. Its location can make it a convenient option for travellers whose flights and accommodation are situated in the southern part of the Paris region.

Booking a private taxi from Paris Orly can help simplify your journey to your hotel, the city centre, or Disneyland Paris. Your journey time will depend on traffic, your destination, and the pickup location.

Beauvais–Tillé Airport (BVA)

Beauvais Airport is located considerably farther from central Paris than CDG and Orly. It is frequently used by low-cost airlines and can be an attractive option for travellers looking for budget-friendly flights.

However, the lower flight price should be considered alongside the additional time and cost of ground transportation. A private transfer from Beauvais can offer a direct journey to Paris, your accommodation, or another airport.

Private Transfers Between Paris Airports

Connecting flights between different Paris airports requires careful planning. CDG, Orly, and Beauvais are separate airports, and travelling between them may take considerably longer than expected during busy traffic periods.
